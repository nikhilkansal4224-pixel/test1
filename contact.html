Help & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers regarding our policies, parking, and dining experience.

What is the dress code at Aurelia? +
We encourage smart casual attire. While jackets are not required for gentlemen, collared shirts and neat trousers are recommended. We kindly ask guests to refrain from athletic sportswear, workout apparel, and beach sandals.
Do you accommodate severe food allergies or vegan diets? +
Absolutely. Our culinary team prepares each component from scratch, allowing us to adapt dishes for gluten intolerance, dairy allergies, vegetarian, and plant-based preferences. Please note dietary details during your reservation.
Are children welcome at the restaurant? +
Well-mannered children who can appreciate a fine dining environment are welcome. We offer simplified seasonal vegetable and pasta preparations upon request. High chairs are available with advance notice.
What is your corkage and cake cutting policy? +
Our corkage fee is $45 per 750ml bottle for bottles not currently featured on our wine list (limit 2 bottles per table). The fee is waived for each bottle purchased from our wine cellar. Cake plating service is $8 per guest.
Where should we park? +
Valet parking is available directly in front of the main entrance at 428 Montgomery Street starting at 5:30 PM Thursday through Saturday ($22 flat evening rate). Alternatively, the St. Mary’s Square Garage is a 3-minute stroll away.
