Since 2018

Born From Reverence For Earth, Sea, and Table

Aurelia began as a quiet conversation between friends who shared a reverence for food in its purest expression. Disenchanted with overly complicated dining theatrics, Chef Julian Morales sought to create a dining haven where unprocessed ingredients and honest craftsmanship take center stage.

Every morning, our kitchen receives deliveries from small-scale growers in Sonoma and coastal fishermen along Half Moon Bay. Our menus are not forced into rigid cycles; rather, they flow with the real micro-seasons of Northern California. When a crop ends, we celebrate its farewell and welcome what is next.

"Real flavor doesn't need disguise. Our duty as chefs is simply to reveal the honest magnificence already present in nature." — Julian Morales, Executive Chef
Foundational Values

The Four Pillars of Aurelia

Every service and every ingredient is held to these four enduring standards.

01

Soil Integrity

100% of our vegetables and herbs come from regenerative, pesticide-free farms that nurture living biodiversity.

02

Transparent Catch

We only purchase wild line-caught fish and sustainably farmed bivalves with certified traceability.

03

Zero Food Waste

From bone broths to house-fermented vinegars, we utilize every portion of our raw harvests through culinary ingenuity.

04

Genuine Warmth

Service that is knowledgeable yet free from pretension. We want every guest to feel welcomed like family.
